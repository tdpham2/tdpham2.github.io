## Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate site traffic, such as which pages are visited, how visitors find the site, approximate location, and browser or device type. This information helps me understand which content is useful and improve the website.

Analytics is disabled by default. Google Analytics loads only after you select **Accept all** or enable the analytics category in the cookie preferences. If you reject analytics, this website does not load the Google Analytics tag.

Google processes analytics information according to its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its explanation of [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
